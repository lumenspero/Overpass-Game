Outline smoothing mock-up

Each map's trimmed shapes, drawn in its own palette as the game draws them. Detail is the share of each shape's imported points that is kept. Below 100%, each outline is first resampled and smoothed (the smoothing reach grows as detail drops: up to 3 tiles at 5%), then simplified to that share of points, then given curved handles. Closed shapes keep their area, so small mountains do not shrink away. Pick a level and the Studio can use it for every shape type.
