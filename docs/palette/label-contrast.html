Label contrast review

Stored label colours (LABEL_COLOURS in packages/render-style), one per terrain family, mode and region kind. Left: the label on its own region. Right: the same label on bare land. Every label reaches 4.5:1 on its own region under every water choice; land ratios under 3:1 are marked in red. Before this change, labels were set to 3:1 against land and fell to 1.0–1.8:1 on light-mode mountains and on dark-mode water for red, orange, blue, indigo and violet.
