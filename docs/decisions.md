## M13 · Joining traffic and lot turns

- **Waiting to join:** a car leaving a house, a lot or a kerb joins only when its lane is clear where it meets it, and no car in that lane will reach that point within `CAR_GAP` of it. Asked by the author, as cars were driving into traffic.
- **Crossing the other lane:** a car that must cross the other lane, to leave or to reach a house or a lot on the far side, waits for cars at or moving toward its crossing point. A car stopped in a queue clear of that point does not hold it. Asked by the author, as cars were crossing traffic.
- **Turning at the lane:** a house is turned into and out of from the car's own lane at the access tile's centre, not from the middle of the road. Asked by the author, as cars took wide turns at houses.
- **Lot inlet columns:** a car leaving a lot crosses its edge at the inlet's centre and turns into its lane a quarter-tile past the node's centre. A car driving in crosses the edge a quarter-tile ahead of where it stops. So no car swings back against its direction, and a car driving in passes a car waiting to leave. Asked by the author, as lot cars swung wide and clipped the inlet.
- **Waiting inside the lot:** a leaving car waits just inside the lot's edge, not on the road. It has given its space up, so a car arriving can take it. This keeps two full lots on one road from locking each other.
- **Patience:** after 5 seconds a waiting car stops waiting for stopped traffic across its path and for cars still to drive on, and a lot car may leave the other way if there is one. After 10 seconds, in gridlock, a lot car merges into a stopped queue in its own lane; it never cuts in on moving traffic. This overlaps briefly, about one lot exit in 50 in the 300-car demo. Chosen so a jam always clears. `approximated`
- **Demo start:** the 300-car demo places its starting cars part way along their trips, one per lane per tile, so it starts full without cars stacked at house entrances.

## M13 · Performance budget (desktop)

- **Sim cost:** a tick with 300 cars took about 5.8 ms in a profile. Three costs dominated: each car scanning every other car for its tile, counting driving cars for every house every tick, and rebuilding a lot's geometry to check it exists. Now about 1.5 ms per tick, with the same results: the 6,000-tick demo run is unchanged (1,946 trips).
- **Tile index:** while cars move, the sim keeps a list of cars per tile, so each car looks only at its own tile. Outside a move (spawning, trips) the sim scans as before.
- **Drawing:** each car is drawn as a fan of triangles from its centre, so Phaser does not triangulate every outline each frame. Headings are worked out with two batched shape calls per frame, instead of two calls per car. Lot cars still follow their lane path.
- **Trace:** two desktop traces, before and after the triangle fans, averaged 33.5 ms and 34.8 ms a frame. The fans gave no measurable gain. The remaining cost is mostly Phaser's WebGL command processing, with some earcut from per-frame fills (the motorway badges, not yet confirmed).
- **Not done:** pooling of car sprites. The 60 fps check on a phone is deferred with mobile testing.
- **Closed:** the author checked the 300-car demo in a desktop browser on 2026-11-04 and signed off the performance as acceptable for this milestone.

## M14 · Part 1: road, lot and house geometry

- **Rounded bends:** a bend is a quarter circle of radius half a tile around the tile corner, so its inside edge is rounded. Car lanes follow the same curve, so cars stay inside the road through bends. Asked by the author, as the sharp inside corners let cars clip the road.
- **Roundabout footprint:** a roundabout is refused where its four orthogonal neighbours hold a building, a lot tile, mountain or water, or another roundabout. Asked by the author, as roundabouts were placed over businesses and houses. The footprint is read from the ring's radius, so it may need adjusting if the ring looks wider on screen.
- **Lot crossing lanes:** entering cars cross the inlet's edge in the right-hand lane and leaving cars in the other lane. The crossing stays inside the inlet's two lanes, never on the road edges. A leaving car's turn never starts behind its node. Asked by the author, as cars drove on the road edge and left from the centre of the inlet.
- **House entry:** a car drives into a house on its own lane of the road, and when the road turns into the house it turns on that lane. Leaving follows the same lane. Asked by the author, as cars entered the middle of the road.
- **Doorframe redraw (fix):** the building layer redraws with the roads, so a house's doorframe tick does not linger after its road changes. A bug fix, not a rule.
